\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
% Gerado a partir de docs/tcc.md por scripts/sincronizar_tcc.py.
This work presents the design and initial development of a contextual evaluation system for digital public services. The research problem concerns linking citizens\textquotesingle{} declared evaluations to the navigation context in which feedback is requested. The work combined bibliographic and regulatory review, Lean Inception, and incremental prototyping, organized around the activities of Design Science Research. A local Python prototype was implemented with an API, simulated pages, operational rules based on clicks and reported duration, a feedback form, and an administrative dashboard. Telemetry provides behavioral indications and does not measure perceived quality. The current functional version records star-based satisfaction, declared attributes, and perceived ease through an optional seven-point CES item. The current satisfaction and CES items were specified after the interface study and have not yet been evaluated with participants. Separately from the functional implementation, eight participants evaluated interface flows in Figma, with automatic click and time records collected through Useberry and a post-session questionnaire. Four participants performed each flow, with artificial-intelligence-assisted allocation described as a draw without an auditable record, and with a redirect popup present in both flows. The results describe task completion, perceived ease, and post-session reports. They do not establish greater participation, more specific feedback-form comments, correct API-trigger behavior, or superiority of contextual feedback. The investigative contribution is the comparative hypothesis and the distinction between behavioral and declared evidence; the technological contribution is the implementation that can connect them. Requirements, traceability, quality criteria, and prospective protocols for citizens, managers, and specialists were specified. Further work requires executing these protocols and calibrating the rules before deployment.

\vspace{\onelineskip}
\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} digital public services; contextual feedback; service evaluation; user experience; Design Science Research.
\end{resumo}
